Phase6A effort calibration (#26): Settings shows a descriptive, user-reported median actual/estimate ratio from explicitly confirmed, frozen per-assignment samples, with "Insufficient data" below three samples and visible exclusions. Actual edits invalidate confirmations; nothing adjusts estimates or plans. See the [sample contract](data-contract.md#effort-calibration-samples-phase-6a-26).

Phase6B workload modes (#27): Home has an explicit, local Standard/Reduced focus mode, defaulting to Standard. Reduced lists overdue and 72-hour required work first and keeps other required and selectable work listed. Verification items, source failures/staleness and calendar coverage limits stay visible in both modes. Selectable work with unknown submission status stays selectable and is also listed for verification. Submitted/graded/excused work stays out of the focus lists even when its source failed, while that failure stays in the health warnings. Focus links for locally archived work open Coursework History. The mode is advisory: it changes no records, approvals or effort samples, infers no capacity from blank calendar time and schedules nothing. See the [mode contract](data-contract.md#workload-modes-phase-6b-27).

Phase6C career feedback ordering (#28): Career Roles and People each list items the user saved first ("Saved by you") and dismissed items last with Restore, otherwise in source order with stable ties. Only each item's own explicit status counts; nothing is inferred for other items and editorial scores are unused. Dismissed items now stay in Roles/People (last, with Restore) instead of being excluded; the other exclusions (applied roles; pending/connected/contacted people; source-removed items unless a saved role) and source-failure retention are unchanged. Eligibility checks are unchanged and still skip missing profile facts (no `usSponsorshipRequired` or no profile: no US sponsorship gap; no start year on role and profile: no start-year gap). Roles therefore shows a visible "not assessed" banner and per-card labels for those cases, which never mean eligible. See the [ordering contract](data-contract.md#career-feedback-ordering-phase-6c-28).

Phase6D weekly evidence trends (#29): Settings → Weekly trends compares two explicitly dated weeks (Monday–Sunday in the snapshot timezone, by source `generatedAt`; import time shown separately) through a read-only, bounded `GET /api/trends`. It shows observed required-work counts by status and per-source health only. Weeks without observations say "No observations", never 0. Records from failed/unavailable sources, archived records and duplicate imports are not counted as fresh. Any non-ok or unreported source, different source sets in the two latest observations, retained evidence the bounded read did not return, a week reaching the 30-day retention horizon, or a truncated read makes the pair "Not comparable", with no change shown. Weeks whose evidence was not read say "No observations read", not "No observations". "No observations" only means none was found within the bounded reads (500 rows, the 1,000 most recent imports and 30-day retention); it is missing data, not proof that nothing was collected. Snapshot pruning and the schema are unchanged. No effort, completion times or work sessions are inferred. See the [trends contract](data-contract.md#weekly-evidence-trends-phase-6d-29).
